# The Odds of Winning Solitaire, Backed with Data

- **Citation key:** `taparia2024odds`
- **Author:** Neal Taparia
- **Year:** 2024 (updated 24 February)
- **Publication:** Solitaired blog
- **Source:** [Original article](https://solitaired.com/odds-of-winning-solitaire)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-29

## Summary

Solitaired reports observed player win rates from millions of games. This is a likely match for the remembered games-site dataset, although its draw-three figure is 11.1%, rather than approximately 13%.

## Key findings

| Variant | Games | Wins | Reported win rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Draw-three | 1,429,916 | 158,382 | 11.1% |
| Draw-one | 2,898,974 | 955,805 | 33.0% |

The article describes random games sampled over a few days.

## Conditions and limitations

Exact sampling dates, unique-player counts, and treatment of abandonment, restarts, hints, and undo are unspecified. These are platform results, not a population-wide human average or a peer-reviewed experiment. They measure observed play rather than the proportion of winnable deals.

## Relevance to Solitaire Odds

**Our interpretation:** useful context for ordinary play, but a direct human-versus-agent comparison requires matching rules, assistance, deal sampling, and the definition of a counted game.
