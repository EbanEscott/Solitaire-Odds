## Comparing studies

- **Full-information solvability** measures deals with a winning sequence when all card locations are known. It provides an estimated ceiling for hidden-information play under matching rules and deal distributions.
- **Player performance** measures one policy under a particular observation interface and budget. A lost game need not be unwinnable.
- **Human platform results** depend on the players, deal selection, assistance, and how unfinished games are counted.

Match draw size, stock passes, worrying back, partial tableau moves, stock visibility, memory, undo, move limits, and deal sampling before comparing results. Preserve whether confidence intervals are 95% or 99%.

Our next research steps are to select a reproducible hidden-information baseline, test stock-observation assumptions, and investigate learned search guidance and complete-game model evaluations. Full-information solvers could provide deal labels to separate unwinnable games from player failures. This reading list does not establish novelty or a ranking against our engine.
