# Literature

Keep reading notes, thematic reviews, and a shared bibliography here. Add `references.bib` when the first sources are recorded, and use consistent citation keys across notes and paper drafts.

For each source, record its title, authors, year, DOI or stable URL, main contribution, method, limitations, and relevance to our [research questions](../questions.md). Distinguish the authors' findings from our interpretation.

Link to source papers from notes. Group sources into thematic review documents as research questions develop.
