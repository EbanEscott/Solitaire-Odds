# What's the Real Klondike Win Rate? Our Data Has an Answer

## Summary

TrySolitaire reports player results for February 2026, with abandoned games included as losses. Its dataset is smaller than Solitaired's, but the denominator is clearer.

## Key findings

| Variant | Game starts | Wins | Reported win rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Draw-three | 1,803 | 160 | 8.9% |
| Draw-one | 6,653 | 2,017 | 30.3% |

The reporting period is 1–28 February 2026. Players use undo.

## Conditions and limitations

These figures describe activity on one platform. The accompanying discussion does not consistently distinguish theoretical draw-one and draw-three solvability; cite the telemetry separately from that discussion. Counting abandonment as a loss and allowing undo affect comparison with automated players.

## Relevance to Solitaire Odds

**Our interpretation:** a second source for human-play context and a useful reminder to define the denominator in our evaluations. Differences from Solitaired do not establish a skill difference between player populations.
