# Research Questions

Capture questions here as they emerge. These entries are research proposals, not established findings.

For each question, record:

- **Question and motivation:** What do we want to understand, and why?
- **Hypothesis:** What outcome do we predict?
- **Related work:** Links to literature notes and the potential gap.
- **Evidence needed:** Comparisons, controls, metrics, and limitations to address.
- **Status:** Proposed, investigating, supported, inconclusive, or rejected.
- **Evidence links:** Working notes, experiment write-ups, and relevant runs.
