# Research

Research questions, literature reviews, experiment write-ups, and publication drafts for Solitaire Odds.

- [Questions](questions.md) — questions, hypotheses, and evidence needed.
- [Literature](literature/README.md) — reading notes and bibliography.
- [Experiments](experiments/README.md) — curated methods, findings, and limitations.
- [Papers](papers/README.md) — candidate contributions, outlines, and manuscripts.

The root [experiments workspace](../experiments/README.md) remains the home for long-running experiments, orchestration, notebooks, and working notes. Link to those records from research write-ups rather than duplicating them. Game logic and player implementations live in `engine/`; model implementations and training primitives live in `neural-network/`.

Keep observations distinct from hypotheses and supported claims. Connect each proposed publication claim to the relevant literature and reproducible experimental evidence.
